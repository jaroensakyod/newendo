Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I customize the starter site after importing it?

    Absolutely! After importing, you can tweak everything – layouts, colours, fonts, and content – using the WordPress Customizer or your favourite page builder.

  • Do I need to install any plugins to use a Blocksy starter site?

    Some starter templates might recommend plugins like a form builder or a page builder, but your site will still work seamlessly regardless of whether you install them.

  • Will importing a starter site overwrite my current content?

    Yes, importing a starter site will replace your existing content. To avoid losing important data, it’s best to import on a fresh install or back up your site beforehand.

  • Do starter website templates support e-commerce features?

    Absolutely. Blocksy’s starter sites are fully customisable, allowing you to change colors, fonts, layouts, and content to perfectly match your brand. You don’t need to touch any code – just use the intuitive WordPress Customizer or your preferred page builder.
